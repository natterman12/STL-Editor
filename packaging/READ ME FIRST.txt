STL Editor (portable)
=====================

CAD-style editing for STL, 3MF and OBJ files: sketch on a face and extrude or cut, drill
holes (with presets for heat-set inserts, screws, nuts and magnets), split big parts to fit
your print bed with alignment pins, mirror, hollow, repair, sculpt and more.

Starting it
-----------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you like (Desktop, Documents, a USB stick...).
   Don't run it from inside the zip.
2. Double-click "STL Editor.exe".

The first time, Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". The app isn't code-signed,
which is normal for small free tools. Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

Needs Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Nothing gets installed and no admin rights are needed.
Your settings are kept in %APPDATA%\STL Editor.

Quick start
-----------
- Drag an STL onto the window (or File > Open).
- Double-click a flat face to sketch on it, draw a rectangle or circle, then click
  "Finish & Extrude", choose Cut and press OK.
- Double-click any step in the timeline at the bottom to change it later.
- File > Export STL / 3MF when you're done.

"User guide.md" has every tool and keyboard shortcut.

Notes
-----
- Your work is autosaved every minute; after a crash, the next start offers to recover it.
- When a new version is posted, a small notice appears with a Download button.
- View > Units switches to inches; View > Theme has a dark mode.
- The "Send to Slicer" buttons work if you have Bambu Studio or OrcaSlicer installed in
  the usual place. "To STEP" needs SolidCraft.
- Licences for the open-source parts are in the LICENSES folder.
